The [pending list](https://www.packomania.com/pending-requests.html) states 17 October 2026. These are candidate counts, not distinct improved N values, and the ranges do not establish which specific N have stronger pending witnesses.

The 1,813 entry was already present in our original archived `sources/packomania-pending.html`; this corrects an incomplete earlier summary, rather than establishing that the queue changed during this session. The existing 557 range includes N=500. Novelty is unresolved even for a successful count outside Aletheia's three named counts.
