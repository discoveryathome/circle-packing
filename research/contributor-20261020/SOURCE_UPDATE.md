## Additional independent-search context

[ZeroThesis’s large-N square challenge](https://zerothesis.com/challenges/circle-packing-square-large?tab=leaderboard) has a different leaderboard from the small-N challenge in the initial audit. Its [public journal](https://zerothesis.com/api/problems/circle-packing-square-large/journal) identifies N=3000 and 9996 as its improved cases, against a 6 September snapshot. It does not claim an N=500 improvement in that journal. The aggregate ratio is therefore not evidence of a stronger witness for this session’s five counts. These larger-count contributions are already discussed in the original catalogue audit.

[The Abandoned Shelf](https://henrywhelan.com/research/abandoned-shelf.html), dated 7 August 2026, reports AI-assisted searches in semicircles, circular quadrants and right triangles. It is outside this pilot’s square-container objective. Its stated use of 40-digit ordinary arithmetic is not an interval certificate; the article also attributes gains mainly to standard search with more compute. We do not import its claimed records or infer that those containers still have untouched headroom today. It provides useful context for why a catalogue gain alone does not demonstrate a special advantage from agent reasoning.

The Aletheia findings page links a raster PNG illustration, not a coordinate SVG or numerical witness. An illustration cannot replace a feasible-coordinate certificate.
