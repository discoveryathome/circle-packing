## Evidence and interpretation

The earlier contributor trial produced meaningful local gains at 500,700,800;
subsequent stronger-start checks mostly stalled or repeated a single polishing
refinement. These numerical results are unchanged. The new working baseline removes
those old gains from the set of easy incoming targets. The explorer is presentation
of existing evidence, not a new research result.

The serial study is **not run**. Its advance gate requires meaningful gains in at
least two of three sessions, across at least two counts, including a later-session
improvement beyond its same-start full-budget polish control. This is a pragmatic
product threshold, not a claim about statistical significance, donor success rate,
agent superiority or record novelty. Full sessions and independent onboarding remain
outstanding even if a software smoke check passes.
